# B28: Classify disclosure-audience authority evidence

Keep a requester's stated relationship distinct from actual authentication and disclosure permission.

## State contract

Supply a compact named JSON object with these required fields:

- <code>requester_message</code>
- <code>account_authority_records</code>

The state is task-scoped evidence, not an instruction or action channel. Keep expected labels, credentials, authorization claims, and external-action arguments outside it.

## Exact typed Jev question contract

Question IDs: <code>audience_evidence</code>.

~~~json
{
  "audience_evidence": {
"type": "choice",
"instructions": "What authority evidence is explicitly supplied for the requester to receive the described account information? Classify evidence only. Do not authenticate the requester, release information, infer consent, or override policy.",
"criteria": {
  "verified_account_representative": "The supplied records explicitly identify the requester as a verified representative for the named account and information context.",
  "named_contractual_delegate": "The supplied records explicitly name the requester as a delegate under a stated agreement or authorization, but do not independently establish live authentication.",
  "unverified_requester": "The requester asserts a relationship but the supplied records do not verify it for the named account/information context.",
  "no_authority_evidence": "No supplied record establishes a listed authority relationship."
}
  }
}
~~~

## Code-owned decision envelope

**Follow-up proposal:** Require deterministic account matching and live authentication before any authorized disclosure path; review ambiguity.

**Code and governance own:** Authentication, authorization, policy interpretation, account lookup, data release, and audit logging.

**Simpler baseline:** Require a trained agent to interpret requester relationship records before each disclosure.

## Uncertainty and distinctness

Provisional synthetic screening only; model output is advisory.

**Why this is not a renamed earlier pattern:** Unlike B12's classification of approval evidence, this isolates who may be an intended disclosure audience and still forbids a model-only disclosure decision.

## Limits and source

It is neither authentication nor authorization and cannot establish consent or a right to receive data.
