# LC04 — What a term expression describes

[Contracts catalog]({{ '/legal-contracts/' | relative_url }}) · [Evaluation and limitations]({{ '/legal-contracts/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) · [Kernel reference module](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Seabass-up/jev-workflow-patterns/main/skills/jev-question-kernel-catalog/references/legal-contracts.md)

Classify whether a code-extracted term expression describes a fixed term, an automatic renewal, or a right to terminate for convenience, leaving date arithmetic to code.

This is a proposed, bounded text judgment. Consult the [evaluation record]({{ '/legal-contracts/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) for observed results; the fixture labels below are intended outputs.

## Proposed benefit

Fills the term column of the contract register from the expression itself, without the model computing an end date or a notice deadline.

This is a proposed benefit, not an observed outcome. All fixtures on this page are synthetic.

## Required named state

Supply the following named fields as the evidence packet:

- `term_expression`
- `clause_text`

Code deterministically returns `unknown` without invoking Jev when any required named field is absent or empty; the model is not the required-field gate. The live missing-input case below probes the contract wording, not that gate.

## Complete synthetic example

This is fixture `LC04-4`, from the **challenge** split. Its author received the question contract without the design fixtures or model results; the expected label is an authored interpretation of the contract, not a Jev prediction.

```json
{
  "term_expression": "for an initial period of thirty-six months from the Effective Date, at the end of which it shall expire without renewal unless both parties have executed a written extension before that date",
  "clause_text": "This Agreement shall continue for an initial period of thirty-six months from the Effective Date, at the end of which it shall expire without renewal unless both parties have executed a written extension before that date. Any extension shall be on the terms stated in the extension instrument and shall not be implied from continued performance or from acceptance of fees after expiry."
}
```

Expected label: `fixed_term`.

The expression states a definite period after which the agreement ends; it continues only if both parties affirmatively sign an extension, which is the opposite of a term that renews on its own unless a party acts, and it grants no right to end the agreement early.

## Code-owned responsibilities

Code extracts candidate expressions with their offsets, resolves every date, period, and notice deadline from the expression in code, and records the clause number; the label never states when the agreement ends.

The judgment is advisory. Code and the responsible person retain control of exact identifiers, data access, state changes, and consequential verification.

## Bounded follow-up

One re-check with the next candidate expression when the label is unknown and code extracted more than one.

## Verification recipe

Compare labels with the term column of an existing contract register; review every expression the register records differently.

This describes required verification work, not checks already performed.

## Source inspiration

- [Wikipedia: Termination for convenience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Termination_for_convenience) (LC-S3). Describes a termination for convenience clause as one that lets a party end a contract without establishing the other party's default, and notes standard forms that allow termination at any time by written notice. Motivates LC04 (what a term expression describes) and LC07 (whether a notice provision states its method and recipient). Motivation, not evidence of Jev performance.

These sources motivate the design problem and its boundaries. They do not demonstrate Jev performance on this question.
