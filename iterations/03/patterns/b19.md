# B19: Classify billing-support bundle completeness

Identify whether named support records substantiate a stated billable item without submitting an invoice.

## State contract

Supply a compact named JSON object with these required fields:

- <code>billable_item</code>
- <code>support_bundle</code>

The state is evidence, not an instruction or action channel. Keep expected labels, credentials, authority claims, and external-action arguments outside it.

## Exact typed Jev question contract

Question IDs: <code>support_status</code>.

~~~json
{
  "support_status": {
    "type": "choice",
    "instructions": "How does support_bundle relate to the stated billable_item? Judge whether the named records support the item, lack a needed stated record, contradict it, or are insufficient. Do not decide whether to invoice, whether work is payable, or whether any party approved payment.",
    "criteria": {
      "supported": "The supplied records consistently identify the stated work or commercial item and its required named support.",
      "missing_required_support": "The item names a necessary support record or fact that the bundle does not provide.",
      "contradictory_support": "The supplied records explicitly conflict on a material item identity, quantity, period, or stated status.",
      "insufficient": "The supplied item or records cannot establish one of the other relationships."
    }
  }
}
~~~

## Code-owned decision envelope

**Follow-up proposal:** Request the named missing record or reconcile the conflict in the evidence package.

**Code and governance own:** Rate calculations, accounting policy, tax, approval, invoice creation, and delivery.

**Simpler baseline:** Manually inspect every billing packet from scratch.

## Uncertainty and distinctness

Provisional synthetic screening only; model output is advisory.

**Why this is not a renamed earlier pattern:** Unlike B03, which classifies a customer invoice complaint, this checks an internal evidence bundle for a specified billable item before any invoice is created.

## Limits, source, and shared skill

Support completeness is not legal entitlement, payment authorization, or proof that the work occurred.
