# Inspiration, not a performance claim

The design pass read these public pages. Each summary describes the design need, not Jev's effectiveness. No source is represented as endorsing this catalog; all contracts and scenarios are task-authored adaptations. The SHA-256 binds the page text as fetched on the listed date, not a claim that the page is unchanged since.

## SA-S1: Wikipedia: Sales

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sales) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `f7b39f1e553673a1…`

Encyclopedic overview of selling as a staged process, naming presentation and demonstration, handling objections, and closing as stages in which prospects raise questions or concerns. Motivates SA01, SA02, SA03, SA06, SA07, and SA08 as classifications of what prospect text asks or states at those stages. Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.

## SA-S2: Wikipedia: Sales process engineering

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sales_process_engineering) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `82376e6b1e5e1715…`

Describes designing a repeatable sales process from lead generation through closing and follow-up, with qualification criteria such as budget, authority, need, and timeline, standardised pipeline phases, and CRM pipeline management. Motivates SA03 (stated buying signals), SA05 and SA07 (follow-up and next-step records), and the pipeline framing of SA01 and SA02. Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [SA01]({{ '/sales/patterns/sa01/' | relative_url }}), [SA02]({{ '/sales/patterns/sa02/' | relative_url }}), [SA03]({{ '/sales/patterns/sa03/' | relative_url }}), [SA05]({{ '/sales/patterns/sa05/' | relative_url }}), [SA07]({{ '/sales/patterns/sa07/' | relative_url }})

## SA-S3: Wikipedia: Request for proposal

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Request_for_proposal) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `aa7e2042d305b51f…`

Describes requests for proposals as documents that communicate a buyer's requirements to prospective suppliers and solicit proposals that are then evaluated. Motivates SA04 as a reading of how a proposal section responds to one stated requirement; whether the requirement is actually met stays with the proposal owner. Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [SA04]({{ '/sales/patterns/sa04/' | relative_url }})

## SA-S4: Wikipedia: Discounts and allowances

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discounts_and_allowances) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `bf6b654593baa54e…`

Catalogues kinds of discounts by what they reward: prompt or annual payment, trade function, quantity, seasonal timing, and customer characteristics such as educational or nonprofit status. Motivates SA06 as a classification of the kind of discount requested against a supplied policy text; every percentage and threshold is compared in code. Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [SA06]({{ '/sales/patterns/sa06/' | relative_url }})

## SA-S5: Wikipedia: Competitor analysis

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Competitor_analysis) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `c1ad9d836d97d01e…`

Describes competitor analysis as assessing the strengths, weaknesses, and positioning of current and potential competitors to inform strategy. Motivates SA08 as a reading of what a competitor mention in a prospect message does, so approved comparison material rather than the model supplies any claim about a competitor. Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.
