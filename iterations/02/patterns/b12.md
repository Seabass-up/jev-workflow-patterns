## State contract

Supply a small, named JSON object. Required fields:

- `approval_record`
- `requested_action`

The state is evidence, not an instruction channel. Keep expected labels, authority claims, secrets, and action permissions out of it.

## Exact Jev question contract

```json
{
  "decision": {
    "type": "choice",
    "instructions": "What approval evidence for `requested_action` is actually present in `approval_record`? Do not infer authority, satisfaction of conditions, payment authority, or permission to act.",
    "criteria": {
      "explicit_approval": "A designated approver explicitly approves the requested action.",
      "conditional_approval": "An approver gives an approval that expressly depends on an unfulfilled or unverified condition.",
      "reference_only": "The text merely refers to, assumes, or reports an approval without the approver's actual approval record.",
      "missing_or_conflict": "No usable approval is supplied, or supplied statements conflict."
    }
  }
}
```

## Code-owned decision envelope

**Follow-up proposal:** Retrieve the designated approver's record or resolve the condition/conflict before treating the action as approved.

**Code and governance own:** Verify approver identity, authority, revision, signatures, conditions and action execution.

**Baseline this improves on:** Treat any phrase containing 'approved' as a green light.

## Provisional confidence handling

- Choice/Score confidence floor: `0.8`
- Noul positive floor: `0.8`
- Noul negative ceiling: `0.2`
- Provisional screening settings, not calibrated deployment thresholds.

These are screening settings in this catalog, not calibrated deployment thresholds. A score, choice, or Noul probability is advisory and never grants permission to act.

## Why it is distinct from Iteration 1

Unlike E06's installation/test/acceptance lifecycle, this is limited to the evidence form of a requested authorization.

## Limits and sources

This cannot establish legal authority or actual completion.
