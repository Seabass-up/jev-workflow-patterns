# MK06 — Competitor mention is a comparative claim or a neutral reference

[Marketing catalog]({{ '/marketing/' | relative_url }}) · [Evaluation and limitations]({{ '/marketing/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) · [Kernel reference module](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Seabass-up/jev-workflow-patterns/main/skills/jev-question-kernel-catalog/references/marketing.md)

Classify how copy refers to a named competitor: as the object of a comparison, as a neutral reference, only as part of a generic comparison, or not at all.

This is a proposed, bounded text judgment. Consult the [evaluation record]({{ '/marketing/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) for observed results; the fixture labels below are intended outputs.

## Proposed benefit

Surfaces comparative claims for substantiation and review before publication, without deciding whether any comparison is fair, accurate, or lawful.

This is a proposed benefit, not an observed outcome. All fixtures on this page are synthetic.

## Required named state

Supply the following named fields as the evidence packet:

- `competitor_name`
- `copy_text`

Code deterministically returns `unknown` without invoking Jev when any required named field is absent or empty; the model is not the required-field gate. The live missing-input case below probes the contract wording, not that gate.

## Policy recorded with its option count

Review when confidence is below 0.8. With 5 options, that floor is equivalent to a top probability of 0.84; a threshold is not portable to a contract with a different option count. These values are provisional workflow policy, not calibration.

## Complete synthetic example

This is fixture `MK06-4`, from the **challenge** split. Its author received the question contract without the design fixtures or model results; the expected label is an authored interpretation of the contract, not a Jev prediction.

```json
{
  "competitor_name": "Northbeam",
  "copy_text": "Moving to Sundial from Northbeam takes one click: our importer carries over every project, board, and comment. 'Northbeam kept crashing on us during quarterly planning; Sundial has not gone down once in eight months,' says Ada Ferrin, operations lead at Kestrel Labs. Sundial also connects to Ledgerly and Inkwell inboxes out of the box."
}
```

Expected label: `comparative_claim`.

The migration sentence on its own would be a neutral reference, and an attributed quotation counts as neutral only when it carries no assertion of superiority or inferiority; this quotation asserts that Northbeam is worse (it crashes) and Sundial better. Because a comparative claim applies, the contract's stated order puts comparative_claim ahead of neutral_reference.

## Code-owned responsibilities

Code supplies the competitor list one name at a time, matches names and product lines exactly before inference, and routes comparative_claim to substantiation and legal review with the copy offsets. The label never decides whether a comparison may run.

The judgment is advisory. Code and the responsible person retain control of exact identifiers, data access, state changes, and consequential verification.

## Bounded follow-up

None; comparative_claim goes to review and no other question is asked.

## Verification recipe

Compare labels with the legal reviewer's log of comparative claims over a quarter of campaigns; measure comparative claims the label missed first.

This describes required verification work, not checks already performed.

## Source inspiration

- [FTC: Advertising FAQ's: A Guide for Small Business](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business) (MK-S1). Explains that advertisers need a reasonable basis of objective evidence for claims before running an ad, that satisfied-customer letters are not enough for claims requiring objective evidence, and that comparative advertising naming a competitor is permitted when truthful and substantiated. Motivates MK01 as a reading of whether a claim matches its supplied evidence and MK06 as a reading of whether a competitor mention is comparative; this page is motivation for the question design, not evidence of Jev performance, and the reasonable-basis and legality determinations stay with people.

These sources motivate the design problem and its boundaries. They do not demonstrate Jev performance on this question.
