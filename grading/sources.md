# Inspiration, not a performance claim

The design pass read these public pages. Each summary describes the design need, not Jev's effectiveness. No source is represented as endorsing this catalog; all contracts and scenarios are task-authored adaptations. The SHA-256 binds the page text as fetched on the listed date, not a claim that the page is unchanged since.

## GR-S1: Wikipedia: Rubric (academic)

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rubric_(academic)) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `f08fac7515e8b0e8…`

Describes a scoring rubric as a set of criteria, each with levels of quality and a descriptor for each level. Motivates GR02 (evidence for one criterion at a time), GR07 (whether adjacent level descriptors state a boundary), and the per-criterion framing of GR01 and GR05. Motivation for the patterns, not evidence of Jev performance.

## GR-S2: GOV.UK: Reducing teacher workload: Marking Policy Review Group report

[Read the source](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/reducing-teacher-workload-marking-policy-review-group-report) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `ea5a764737bbaaf7…`

A government review group's report on reducing the workload of marking, setting out that effective marking should be meaningful, manageable, and motivating. Motivates GR03 (whether a comment gives the student something to do) and the collection's aim of directing a teacher's reading time; also the reading-order use of GR01, GR05, and GR08. Motivation, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [GR01]({{ '/grading/patterns/gr01/' | relative_url }}), [GR03]({{ '/grading/patterns/gr03/' | relative_url }}), [GR05]({{ '/grading/patterns/gr05/' | relative_url }}), [GR08]({{ '/grading/patterns/gr08/' | relative_url }})

## GR-S3: Wikipedia: Paraphrase

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paraphrase) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `445e017f3e6188a7…`

Defines a paraphrase as a restatement of a text in other words that keeps its meaning, as distinct from quotation. Motivates GR06 as a reading of the wording and structure relation between a student passage and a source passage; the acceptability decision stays with the teacher. Motivation, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [GR06]({{ '/grading/patterns/gr06/' | relative_url }})

## GR-S4: Wikipedia: Citation

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citation) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `47b289ae5362c6fa…`

Describes a citation as an in-text reference that points to an entry in a work's reference list and surveys parenthetical and note-based systems. Motivates GR04 as a check for whether quoted material carries such a reference; format rules and reference-list matching stay in code. Motivation, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [GR04]({{ '/grading/patterns/gr04/' | relative_url }})

## GR-S5: UNC Writing Center: Conclusions

[Read the source](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/conclusions/) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `f9ec32f4c3e2b37e…`

A university writing center handout on what a conclusion should do for a reader, with strategies to avoid such as ending on a rephrased thesis with nothing added or introducing a new idea or subtopic in the conclusion. Motivates GR08 (concludes, restates only, or introduces new material) and the on-topic framing of GR01. Motivation, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [GR08]({{ '/grading/patterns/gr08/' | relative_url }})
