# CE01 — Memo states the decision being asked for

[Executive catalog]({{ '/executive/' | relative_url }}) · [Evaluation and limitations]({{ '/executive/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) · [Kernel reference module](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Seabass-up/jev-workflow-patterns/main/skills/jev-question-kernel-catalog/references/executive.md)

Classify whether a memo names the specific decision it asks its executive reader to make, or only supplies background.

This is a proposed, bounded text judgment. Consult the [evaluation record]({{ '/executive/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) for observed results; the fixture labels below are intended outputs.

## Proposed benefit

Lets a chief of staff return memos that ask for a decision without saying which one, before they take a slot on the agenda.

This is a proposed benefit, not an observed outcome. All fixtures on this page are synthetic.

## Required named state

Supply the following named fields as the evidence packet:

- `memo_text`

Code deterministically returns `unknown` without invoking Jev when any required named field is absent or empty; the model is not the required-field gate. The live missing-input case below probes the contract wording, not that gate.

## Exact question JSON

```json
{
  "decision": {
    "type": "choice",

Classify whether `memo_text` states the decision it asks its reader to make. A memo states the decision when it names a specific action to approve or reject, or specific options to choose among, anywhere in the text, even after long background. A memo that says a decision or direction from the reader is needed, or asks for thoughts on a situation, without naming the action or the options leaves the decision unspecified. A memo that reports status or context, or asks for something other than a decision, requests no decision. If a memo both states a specific decision and asks for general thoughts, select decision_stated. Do not judge whether the decision is a good one.     "criteria": {

      "no_decision_requested": "The memo reports status or context, or asks for something other than a decision, and requests no decision.",
      "unknown": "Required evidence is missing, contradictory, or does not support a determinate classification."
    }
  }
```

## Policy recorded with its option count

Review when confidence is below 0.8. With 4 options, that floor is equivalent to a top probability of 0.85; a threshold is not portable to a contract with a different option count. These values are provisional workflow policy, not calibration.

## Complete synthetic example

This is fixture `CE01-4`, from the **challenge** split. Its author received the question contract without the design fixtures or model results; the expected label is an authored interpretation of the contract, not a Jev prediction.

```json
{
  "memo_text": "To: Dana Okonkwo, COO (d.okonkwo@tallisgroup.example). Subject: Decision record for the Tallis warehouse lease. After two months of review, the facilities team has made its decision: we are renewing the Tallis lease for three years rather than relocating to the Norrell site or consolidating into the Pendry hub. Both alternatives were weighed and rejected on access and timing grounds, and the decision was signed off within my delegated authority on Tuesday. I am not asking you to decide anything here. What I need from you is to forward this record to the regional leads before Friday's operations call so they hear it from you rather than from the landlord."
}
```

Expected label: `no_decision_requested`.

The memo names options and repeats the word decision, but the options were already chosen by the author under delegated authority and are not put to the reader; the only request is to forward the record, which the contract treats as asking for something other than a decision.

## Code-owned responsibilities

Code places decision_stated memos on the decision docket, returns decision_unspecified memos to the author with a request to state the ask, and files the rest; the chief of staff sets the agenda and the label approves nothing.

The judgment is advisory. Code and the responsible person retain control of exact identifiers, data access, state changes, and consequential verification.

## Bounded follow-up

One re-check after the author revises a memo labeled decision_unspecified.

## Verification recipe

Compare labels with how the chief of staff actually triaged a quarter of memos; review first every decision_stated memo the executive team could not act on.

This describes required verification work, not checks already performed.

## Source inspiration

- [Wikipedia: Memorandum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memorandum) (CE-S1). Describes memos and briefing notes as documents that bring a problem to notice, are marked either for information or for decision, and typically give background, identify options, and advocate a recommended one. Motivates CE01 and CE05 as readings of whether a memo states its decision and what kind of response it asks for. This is motivation for the questions, not evidence of Jev performance.

These sources motivate the design problem and its boundaries. They do not demonstrate Jev performance on this question.
