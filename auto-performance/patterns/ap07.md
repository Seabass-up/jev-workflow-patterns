# AP07 — Dyno report states a measured result with conditions, or a claim

[Auto performance catalog]({{ '/auto-performance/' | relative_url }}) · [Evaluation and limitations]({{ '/auto-performance/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) · [Kernel reference module](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Seabass-up/jev-workflow-patterns/main/skills/jev-question-kernel-catalog/references/auto-performance.md)

Classify whether a dyno report's figures are measured results with stated conditions, measured results without conditions, or claims and estimates.

This is a proposed, bounded text judgment. Consult the [evaluation record]({{ '/auto-performance/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) for observed results; the fixture labels below are intended outputs.

## Proposed benefit

Separates measured results from advertised or estimated figures before numbers are compared, so a customer is not shown a gain computed from an estimate.

This is a proposed benefit, not an observed outcome. All fixtures on this page are synthetic.

## Required named state

Supply the following named fields as the evidence packet:

- `dyno_report`

Code deterministically returns `unknown` without invoking Jev when any required named field is absent or empty; the model is not the required-field gate. The live missing-input case below probes the contract wording, not that gate.

      "claimed_or_estimated": "The figures are given as expected, advertised, estimated, calculated from other figures, or quoted from a manufacturer or vendor, not as a measurement performed on the described vehicle.",

## Policy recorded with its option count

Review when confidence is below 0.8. With 4 options, that floor is equivalent to a top probability of 0.85; a threshold is not portable to a contract with a different option count. These values are provisional workflow policy, not calibration.

## Complete synthetic example

This is fixture `AP07-4`, from the **challenge** split. Its author received the question contract without the design fixtures or model results; the expected label is an authored interpretation of the contract, not a Jev prediction.

```json
{
  "dyno_report": "Customer arrived after installing the vendor's intake and calibration package. The vendor's product page advertises its power figure as recorded on a hub dynamometer with a standard correction applied, in fourth gear on premium fuel. We ran three pulls on our dyno and the best pull's power and torque are printed on the attached sheet as our measured result. Session logged by the afternoon operator in bay two."
}
```

Expected label: `measured_without_conditions`.

The report contains both a vendor claim and a shop measurement, so the measured result is what gets classified. Every stated condition (hub dyno, correction, gear, fuel) is attached to the vendor's advertised figure, while the shop's own measurement states no condition; the operator and bay are not conditions of the measurement.

## Code-owned responsibilities

Code compares figures, computes gains, applies correction factors, and records the dyno session identifier; it compares one figure with another only when both are labelled as measured.

The judgment is advisory. Code and the responsible person retain control of exact identifiers, data access, state changes, and consequential verification.

## Bounded follow-up

One re-check after the operator adds the session conditions when the label is measured_without_conditions.

## Verification recipe

Compare labels with the dyno operator's classification of report text on past sessions and with the original dyno printouts.

This describes required verification work, not checks already performed.
