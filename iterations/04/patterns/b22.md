# B22: Classify buyer decision-role evidence

Turn a contact's stated role in a purchase into a bounded follow-up branch without assigning authority.

**Contract version 2:** Multiple roles require explicit self-claims from different role categories; several buyer actions in one category remain one role, and job titles do not establish participation. The current cases cover all answer labels; see the evaluation record for synthetic results. This is not production qualification.

## State contract

Supply a compact named JSON object with these required fields:

- <code>message</code>
- <code>contact_context</code>

The state is task-scoped evidence, not an instruction or action channel. Keep expected labels, credentials, authorization claims, and external-action arguments outside it.

## Exact typed Jev question contract

Question IDs: <code>buyer_role</code>.

~~~json
{
  "buyer_role": {
    "type": "choice",
    "instructions": "Which buyer-decision role(s) does the speaker explicitly claim in message? Count a role only when the speaker describes their own participation. Choose multiple_stated_roles only when the speaker explicitly claims roles from two different categories; multiple activities within one category do not count, and mentioning another person's role does not count. Otherwise choose the one explicit role, or not_stated if none is explicit. Do not infer from job titles or authority beyond the stated participation. Do not create a CRM role, contact anyone, or commit a sale.",
    "criteria": {
      "multiple_stated_roles": "The speaker explicitly claims their own participation in at least two different role categories: economic buyer, technical evaluator, operational user, or procurement gatekeeper. Two actions listed under one category are still one role; this takes precedence over the single-role labels.",
      "economic_buyer": "The speaker explicitly says they approve budget, select the supplier, or make the final purchase decision, and does not explicitly claim a second role category.",
      "technical_evaluator": "The speaker explicitly assesses technical fit or recommends a solution, and does not explicitly claim a second role category.",
      "operational_user": "The speaker explicitly says they will use or operate the product/service, and does not explicitly claim a second role category.",
      "procurement_gatekeeper": "The speaker explicitly manages purchasing process, vendor onboarding, or purchase-order requirements, and does not explicitly claim a second role category.",
      "not_stated": "The supplied text does not explicitly state any listed decision role."
    }
  }
}
~~~

## Code-owned decision envelope

**Follow-up proposal:** Ask the sender or account team to confirm an unstated or multiple explicit role; attach the exact message excerpt. Never choose a primary role from job title or confidence alone.

**Code and governance own:** Identity, consent, CRM updates, account ownership, outreach, approvals, pricing, and all external communication.

**Simpler baseline:** Route every prospective-contact message to one general sales queue.

## Uncertainty and distinctness

Provisional synthetic screening only; model output is advisory.

**Why this is not a renamed earlier pattern:** Unlike B09, which identifies an explicitly assigned internal business owner, this classifies an external participant's stated buying role and never assigns ownership.

## Limits and source

A stated role can be stale, strategic, or incomplete and does not establish authority to buy or receive information.
