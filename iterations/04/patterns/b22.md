# B22: Classify buyer decision-role evidence

Turn a contact's stated role in a purchase into a bounded follow-up branch without assigning authority.

## State contract

Supply a compact named JSON object with these required fields:

- <code>message</code>
- <code>contact_context</code>

The state is task-scoped evidence, not an instruction or action channel. Keep expected labels, credentials, authorization claims, and external-action arguments outside it.

## Exact typed Jev question contract

Question IDs: <code>buyer_role</code>.

~~~json
{
  "buyer_role": {
"type": "choice",
"instructions": "What role does the speaker explicitly state that they play in the described buying decision? Read stated participation, not a job-title guess. Do not create a CRM role, infer budget authority, contact another person, or commit a sale.",
"criteria": {
  "economic_buyer": "The speaker explicitly says they approve budget, select the supplier, or make the final purchase decision.",
  "technical_evaluator": "The speaker explicitly says they assess technical fit or recommend a solution but does not claim final purchase authority.",
  "operational_user": "The speaker explicitly says they will use or operate the product/service but does not claim evaluation or purchase authority.",
  "procurement_gatekeeper": "The speaker explicitly says they manage purchasing process, vendor onboarding, or purchase-order requirements without claiming the business selection.",
  "not_stated": "The supplied text does not clearly state one listed decision role."
}
  }
}
~~~

## Code-owned decision envelope

**Follow-up proposal:** Ask the sender or account team to confirm an unstated role; attach the exact message excerpt to any proposed follow-up.

**Code and governance own:** Identity, consent, CRM updates, account ownership, outreach, approvals, pricing, and all external communication.

**Simpler baseline:** Route every prospective-contact message to one general sales queue.

## Uncertainty and distinctness

Provisional synthetic screening only; model output is advisory.

**Why this is not a renamed earlier pattern:** Unlike B09, which identifies an explicitly assigned internal business owner, this classifies an external participant's stated buying role and never assigns ownership.

## Limits and source

A stated role can be stale, strategic, or incomplete and does not establish authority to buy or receive information.
