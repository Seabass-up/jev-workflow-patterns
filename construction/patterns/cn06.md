# CN06 — Trade group a punch-list item is directed at

[Construction catalog]({{ '/construction/' | relative_url }}) · [Evaluation and limitations]({{ '/construction/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) · [Kernel reference module](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Seabass-up/jev-workflow-patterns/main/skills/jev-question-kernel-catalog/references/construction.md)

Classify which of four trade groups must do the work a punch-list item describes, read against the project's own supplied scope descriptions, so code can send it to the right subcontractor.

This is a proposed, bounded text judgment. Consult the [evaluation record]({{ '/construction/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) for observed results; the fixture labels below are intended outputs.

## Proposed benefit

Routes hundreds of punch-list items to the right trade without the superintendent re-reading each one, while cost, fault, and closure stay with people and the tracker.

This is a proposed benefit, not an observed outcome. All fixtures on this page are synthetic.

## Required named state

Supply the following named fields as the evidence packet:

- `punch_item`
- `trade_scopes`

Code deterministically returns `unknown` without invoking Jev when any required named field is absent or empty; the model is not the required-field gate. The live missing-input case below probes the contract wording, not that gate.

## Policy recorded with its option count

Review when confidence is below 0.8. With 6 options, that floor is equivalent to a top probability of 0.8333; a threshold is not portable to a contract with a different option count. These values are provisional workflow policy, not calibration.

## Complete synthetic example

This is fixture `CN06-4`, from the **challenge** split. Its author received the question contract without the design fixtures or model results; the expected label is an authored interpretation of the contract, not a Jev prediction.

```json
{
  "punch_item": "Building-mounted wall pack at the loading dock, north wall, is not illuminating at dusk; the adjacent wall pack comes on normally. Troubleshoot the fixture and its photocell, replace whichever has failed, and verify operation after dark.",
  "trade_scopes": {
    "finishes_and_openings": "Drywall, paint, flooring, ceilings, casework, interior doors, frames, hardware, and interior glazing. Excludes the exterior storefront and entrance doors.",
    "mechanical_and_plumbing": "HVAC equipment, ductwork, diffusers, piping, plumbing fixtures, and the building automation controls. Excludes roof curbs and flashing at rooftop units.",
    "electrical_and_low_voltage": "Interior lighting, receptacles, switches, panels, fire alarm, data cabling, and security devices inside the building line. Site lighting and the exterior building-mounted light fixtures are carried by the site contractor, not this group.",
    "exterior_and_site": "Roofing, flashing, rooftop-unit curbs, siding, exterior sealants, storefront and entrance doors, paving, site concrete, landscaping, site lighting poles and fixtures, and the exterior building-mounted light fixtures including their photocells."
  }
}
```

Expected label: `exterior_and_site`.

The label description lists lighting as an electrical example, but the supplied trade_scopes assigns exterior building-mounted light fixtures and their photocells to the exterior and site group and excludes them from electrical, and the contract states that trade_scopes governs where it differs from the examples.

## Code-owned responsibilities

Code owns the mapping from trade group to the subcontractor and contact on this project, the item number and location, notification, sign-off, back-charges, and closure; the label routes the work only.

The judgment is advisory. Code and the responsible person retain control of exact identifiers, data access, state changes, and consequential verification.

## Bounded follow-up

One re-check after the superintendent adds the location or the defect description when the label is unknown.

## Verification recipe

Compare labels with the trade the superintendent assigned on a completed project's punch list; review every item that was reassigned between subcontractors.

This describes required verification work, not checks already performed.

## Source inspiration

- [Wikipedia: Construction management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Construction_management) (CN-S5). Describes construction management as integrating cost, schedule, quality, safety, and scope, lists quality and safety management, contract administration, and documentation and claims management among its responsibilities, states that project documentation includes diaries, logs, and daily field reports kept as records for dispute resolution, and names the warranty period and closeout as the final phase. Motivates CN02 (daily logs), CN05 (safety observations), CN06 (punch lists as a quality and closeout record), and CN08 (closeout documents); motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.

These sources motivate the design problem and its boundaries. They do not demonstrate Jev performance on this question.
