# CE05 — The memo asks for a decision, information, or alignment

[Executive catalog]({{ '/executive/' | relative_url }}) · [Evaluation and limitations]({{ '/executive/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) · [Kernel reference module](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Seabass-up/jev-workflow-patterns/main/skills/jev-question-kernel-catalog/references/executive.md)

Classify the kind of response a memo asks of its executive reader.

This is a proposed, bounded text judgment. Consult the [evaluation record]({{ '/executive/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) for observed results; the fixture labels below are intended outputs.

## Proposed benefit

Routes memos to the right handling: decisions to the docket, information requests to whoever holds the answer, alignment checks to a quick reply.

This is a proposed benefit, not an observed outcome. All fixtures on this page are synthetic.

## Required named state

Supply the following named fields as the evidence packet:

- `memo_text`

Code deterministically returns `unknown` without invoking Jev when any required named field is absent or empty; the model is not the required-field gate. The live missing-input case below probes the contract wording, not that gate.

## Policy recorded with its option count

Review when confidence is below 0.8. With 5 options, that floor is equivalent to a top probability of 0.84; a threshold is not portable to a contract with a different option count. These values are provisional workflow policy, not calibration.

## Complete synthetic example

This is fixture `CE05-4`, from the **challenge** split. Its author received the question contract without the design fixtures or model results; the expected label is an authored interpretation of the contract, not a Jev prediction.

```json
{
  "memo_text": "Subject: Support queue consolidation and Q3 figures. Two things. First, we are going to merge the Tier 1 and Tier 2 support queues under Priya Castellan starting next month so that escalations stop bouncing between teams; I want to make sure you are comfortable with that direction before I announce it, so please reply if you see a problem with it. Second, could you send me the Q3 vendor spend report and the current headcount by region? I need both for the board pre-read, and Finance says the versions I have are stale. Nothing here needs sign-off; the queue change is within my remit."
}
```

Expected label: `alignment`.

The memo asks the reader to supply figures and documents (information) and also states a plan and asks the reader to confirm comfort or object with no action to approve (alignment); the contract's stated precedence of decision, then alignment, then information selects alignment.

## Code-owned responsibilities

Code routes each label to its handling path and tracks the reply; the executive decides what to answer and the label never answers for them.

The judgment is advisory. Code and the responsible person retain control of exact identifiers, data access, state changes, and consequential verification.

## Bounded follow-up

None; a memo labeled unknown goes to the chief of staff.

## Verification recipe

Compare labels with how the executive office actually handled a quarter of memos; review decision and alignment disagreements first.

This describes required verification work, not checks already performed.

## Source inspiration

- [Wikipedia: Memorandum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memorandum) (CE-S1). Describes memos and briefing notes as documents that bring a problem to notice, are marked either for information or for decision, and typically give background, identify options, and advocate a recommended one. Motivates CE01 and CE05 as readings of whether a memo states its decision and what kind of response it asks for. This is motivation for the questions, not evidence of Jev performance.

These sources motivate the design problem and its boundaries. They do not demonstrate Jev performance on this question.
