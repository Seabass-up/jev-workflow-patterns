# ST08 — Passage relies on something the reader has not been told

[Storytelling catalog]({{ '/storytelling/' | relative_url }}) · [Evaluation and limitations]({{ '/storytelling/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) · [Kernel reference module](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Seabass-up/jev-workflow-patterns/main/skills/jev-question-kernel-catalog/references/storytelling.md)

Check whether a passage depends on information the reader has not yet received in the work.

This is a proposed, bounded text judgment. Consult the [evaluation record]({{ '/storytelling/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) for observed results; the fixture labels below are intended outputs.

<div class="callout warning"><p><strong>Provisional.</strong> At least one screening case disagreed with its authored label or did not complete. The disagreement is preserved on the evaluation page; neither the contract nor the label was changed after inference.</p></div>

## Proposed benefit

Catches references to characters, places, or events that were cut or moved earlier in revision.

This is a proposed benefit, not an observed outcome. All fixtures on this page are synthetic.

## Required named state

Supply the following named fields as the evidence packet:

- `reader_knowledge`
- `passage_text`

Code deterministically returns `unknown` without invoking Jev when any required named field is absent or empty; the model is not the required-field gate. The live missing-input case below probes the contract wording, not that gate.

Classify whether `passage_text` depends on a character, place, object, event, or rule that the list does not include and the passage itself does not introduce. A first mention that the passage explains is an introduction, not a gap. A deliberate mystery the passage clearly frames as unknown is not a gap.     "criteria": {
```json
      "grounded": "Everything the passage relies on is in `reader_knowledge` or is introduced within the passage.",
      "ungrounded_reference": "The passage relies on at least one element that the reader has not been told about and the passage does not introduce.",
      "unknown": "Required evidence is missing, contradictory, or does not support a determinate classification."
    }
  }
}
```

## Policy recorded with its option count

Review when confidence is below 0.8. With 3 options, that floor is equivalent to a top probability of 0.8667; a threshold is not portable to a contract with a different option count. These values are provisional workflow policy, not calibration.

## Complete synthetic example

This is fixture `ST08-4`, from the **challenge** split. Its author received the question contract without the design fixtures or model results; the expected label is an authored interpretation of the contract, not a Jev prediction.

```json
{
  "reader_knowledge": [
    "Bea Corvane runs the rope ferry at Lowmere, a village on a lake.",
    "Her husband Dominic drowned three winters ago; his body was never found.",
    "The ferry has not run since the lake iced over in December.",
    "Bea has one child, a daughter named Tilde, aged nine."
  ],
  "passage_text": "Bea's cousin Aldo — Dominic's side of the family, the one who had gone to sea at fourteen and come home at thirty with a limp and no stories — was standing at the end of the jetty when she came down with the rope. He had been in Lowmere a week and had not once gone near the boathouse at the jetty's end, and nobody in the village would tell her why; she had asked half of them and had stopped asking. 'Ice is going,' Aldo said, without turning. It was, in long grey cracks out toward the far shore, and she thought of Dominic and then made herself think of the rope instead."
}
```

Expected label: `grounded`.

Aldo is absent from reader_knowledge but the passage introduces him on first mention, the boathouse avoidance is a deliberate mystery the passage clearly frames as unknown to Bea and the reader alike, and everything else the passage relies on — Dominic, the ferry rope, the ice — is in the list, so nothing it depends on is an ungrounded reference.

## Code-owned responsibilities

Code builds `reader_knowledge` from the manuscript order, updates it after each passage, and tracks which references were cut in revision.

The judgment is advisory. Code and the responsible person retain control of exact identifiers, data access, state changes, and consequential verification.

## Bounded follow-up

One re-check after code rebuilds `reader_knowledge` from the current chapter order.

## Verification recipe

Reorder or cut chapters in a test manuscript and measure whether the label finds the references that break.

This describes required verification work, not checks already performed.

## Source inspiration

- [Wikipedia: Chekhov's gun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chekhov%27s_gun) (SS-5). Describes the principle that a detail introduced in a story should be used later. Motivates SL06 and SL08.

These sources motivate the design problem and its boundaries. They do not demonstrate Jev performance on this question.
