# Inspiration, not a performance claim

The design pass read these public pages. Each summary describes the design need, not Jev's effectiveness. No source is represented as endorsing this catalog; all contracts and scenarios are task-authored adaptations. The SHA-256 binds the page text as fetched on the listed date, not a claim that the page is unchanged since.

## RE-S1: HUD: Housing Discrimination Under the Fair Housing Act

[Read the source](https://www.hud.gov/program_offices/fair_housing_equal_opp/fair_housing_act_overview) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `a4868b85f3875f26…`

The federal agency's overview of the Fair Housing Act, which prohibits discrimination in the sale, rental, and advertising of housing on the basis of protected characteristics. Motivates the boundary applied across RE01, RE02, RE05, and RE08: every label reads text about a property, a document, or a request and never judges or infers anything about a person. This is motivation for the design, not evidence of Jev performance.

## RE-S2: Wikipedia: Home inspection

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_inspection) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `ac94b2c45c32cede…`

Describes a home inspection as a visual examination of a property's condition delivered as a written report, often tied to a purchase contingency, with findings that range from safety concerns to cosmetic notes. Motivates RE03 as a reading of the severity an inspector's own wording states, and the inspection contingency in RE07. Encyclopedic overview and motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [RE03]({{ '/real-estate/patterns/re03/' | relative_url }}), [RE07]({{ '/real-estate/patterns/re07/' | relative_url }})

## RE-S3: Wikipedia: Lease

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lease) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `78b356592d1157a9…`

Describes a lease as a contract conveying use of property for rent, with terms covering rent, maintenance and repair obligations, permitted use, and termination or renewal. Motivates RE04 (clause topic), RE05 (tenant message routing), and RE08 (maintenance request signs); whether any clause is enforceable stays with counsel. Motivation for the design, not evidence of Jev performance.

## RE-S4: US EPA: Real Estate Disclosures about Potential Lead Hazards

[Read the source](https://www.epa.gov/lead/real-estate-disclosures-about-potential-lead-hazards) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `e1c1dad371a3c223…`

A federal agency's description of the disclosure that sellers and lessors of older housing must provide about known lead-based paint and hazards, using a disclosure form. Motivates RE06 as a check that a completed disclosure makes a statement about a named item; which items a jurisdiction requires, and whether a statement is legally sufficient, stay in code and with counsel. Motivation for the design, not evidence of Jev performance.

## RE-S5: Wikipedia: Real estate contract

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_estate_contract) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `ed08b2420265a472…`

Describes purchase contracts for real property, including identification of the property and its fixtures, and contingencies such as financing, inspection, appraisal, and the sale of another property that condition the buyer's obligation. Motivates RE07 (contingency language), RE01 (a listing claim checked against the property record), and the subjects of RE02 inquiries; no contract term is interpreted by the model. Motivation for the design, not evidence of Jev performance.
