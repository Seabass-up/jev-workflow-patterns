# Inspiration, not a performance claim

The design pass read these public pages. Each summary describes the design need, not Jev's effectiveness. No source is represented as endorsing this catalog; all contracts and scenarios are task-authored adaptations. The SHA-256 binds the page text as fetched on the listed date, not a claim that the page is unchanged since.

## SS-1: Aristotle, Poetics (Project Gutenberg)

[Read the source](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/1974/pg1974.txt) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `3d0100ec20b93c81…`

Plot as the arrangement of incidents in which each event follows from what precedes it, with reversal and recognition as changes of situation. Motivates SL01 and SL06 as tests of whether a scene changes anything and whether a planted element is used.

Used by: [ST01]({{ '/storytelling/patterns/st01/' | relative_url }}), [ST02]({{ '/storytelling/patterns/st02/' | relative_url }}), [ST06]({{ '/storytelling/patterns/st06/' | relative_url }})

## SS-2: Wikipedia: Story structure

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narrative_structure) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `2ccb562848d1a961…`

## SS-3: Wikipedia: Narration

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narration) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `21ddf2897a2e79c5…`

Describes narrative point of view, including limited viewpoints restricted to what one character knows, and narrator reliability. Motivates SL02, SL03, SL08.

Used by: [ST02]({{ '/storytelling/patterns/st02/' | relative_url }}), [ST03]({{ '/storytelling/patterns/st03/' | relative_url }}), [ST08]({{ '/storytelling/patterns/st08/' | relative_url }})

## SS-4: Wikipedia: Show, don't tell

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Show,_don%27t_tell) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `a2b1c7e8dd876655…`

Describes the advice to convey emotion through action and sensation rather than exposition, together with its critics. Motivates SL04, SL05, SL07 as classifications, not rules.

Used by: [ST04]({{ '/storytelling/patterns/st04/' | relative_url }}), [ST05]({{ '/storytelling/patterns/st05/' | relative_url }}), [ST07]({{ '/storytelling/patterns/st07/' | relative_url }})
