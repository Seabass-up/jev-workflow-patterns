# B17: Classify delivery acknowledgement evidence

Separate a receiving record from shipment, exception, and technical-acceptance claims.

## State contract

Supply a compact named JSON object with these required fields:

- <code>delivery_record</code>

The state is evidence, not an instruction or action channel. Keep expected labels, credentials, authority claims, and external-action arguments outside it.

## Exact typed Jev question contract

Question IDs: <code>delivery_evidence</code>.

~~~json
{
  "delivery_evidence": {
    "type": "choice",
    "instructions": "What delivery acknowledgement evidence is actually stated in delivery_record? Classify the logistics evidence only. Do not infer technical acceptance, inventory posting, title transfer, or payment status.",
    "criteria": {
      "received_without_exception": "A recipient or receiving record explicitly acknowledges receipt and states no material delivery exception.",
      "dispatched_only": "The record establishes shipment, carrier handoff, or tracking but not receipt by the intended receiver.",
      "exception_documented": "The record explicitly reports a delivery discrepancy, damage, shortage, refusal, or missing receipt.",
      "absent_or_unclear": "No supplied text establishes one of the other evidence states."
    }
  }
}
~~~

## Code-owned decision envelope

**Follow-up proposal:** Keep the carrier and receiving evidence together; route exceptions to the designated logistics process.

**Code and governance own:** Tracking lookup, quantity reconciliation, inventory, claims, acceptance, and accounting state.

**Simpler baseline:** Treat a shipment notification as delivery confirmation.

## Uncertainty and distinctness

Provisional synthetic screening only; model output is advisory.

**Why this is not a renamed earlier pattern:** Unlike E06, this classifies carrier and receiving evidence for logistics and explicitly excludes installation, testing, and designated technical acceptance.

## Limits, source, and shared skill

A written acknowledgement can still be inaccurate and does not prove physical condition.
