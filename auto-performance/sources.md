# Inspiration, not a performance claim

The design pass read these public pages. Each summary describes the design need, not Jev's effectiveness. No source is represented as endorsing this catalog; all contracts and scenarios are task-authored adaptations. The SHA-256 binds the page text as fetched on the listed date, not a claim that the page is unchanged since.

## AP-S1: Wikipedia: Engine knocking

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engine_knocking) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `02a772abbc696256…`

Describes knocking (detonation) as abnormal combustion outside the flame front, distinguishes it from pre-ignition, and notes knock sensors and timing retard as responses. Motivates AP04 as a text classification of the symptom that a tuning note and code-written reading flags describe. Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [AP04]({{ '/auto-performance/patterns/ap04/' | relative_url }})

## AP-S2: Wikipedia: Dynamometer

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamometer) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `f4d1039f12799e48…`

Describes engine and chassis dynamometers, inertia versus controlled-load testing, drivetrain losses between engine and chassis figures, and why test conditions affect tuning results. Motivates AP07 (measured result with stated conditions versus a claim) and AP08 (a stated measurement as grounds for advice); code compares the figures. Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [AP07]({{ '/auto-performance/patterns/ap07/' | relative_url }}), [AP08]({{ '/auto-performance/patterns/ap08/' | relative_url }})

## AP-S3: Wikipedia: Car tuning

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Car_tuning) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `496d5ae887ad37fc…`

Overviews modifying cars for performance, handling, or appearance: engine and management changes, stiffened suspension, wider tires, better brakes, and aerodynamic and appearance parts, with track, street, and show purposes. Motivates AP01 (system changed), AP02 (stated use), AP03 (fitment of parts to a vehicle), and AP06 (install steps). Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [AP01]({{ '/auto-performance/patterns/ap01/' | relative_url }}), [AP02]({{ '/auto-performance/patterns/ap02/' | relative_url }}), [AP03]({{ '/auto-performance/patterns/ap03/' | relative_url }}), [AP06]({{ '/auto-performance/patterns/ap06/' | relative_url }})

## AP-S4: GOV.UK: Change vehicle details on a V5C registration certificate

[Read the source](https://www.gov.uk/change-vehicle-details-registration-certificate) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `cc76f10efbfee19d…`

A registration authority's public list of vehicle changes that must be declared, such as engine, cylinder capacity, fuel type, and chassis or bodyshell, with like-for-like replacements reported separately and some changes the authority may inspect. Motivates AP05 as a reading of what a supplied rules text says about a described change; the authority's rules stay in code and with the authority. Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [AP05]({{ '/auto-performance/patterns/ap05/' | relative_url }})

## AP-S5: Wikipedia: Anecdotal evidence

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anecdotal_evidence) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `b0c65bae587b3b00…`
