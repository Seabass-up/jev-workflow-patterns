# Inspiration, not a performance claim

The design pass read these public pages. Each summary describes the design need, not Jev's effectiveness. No source is represented as endorsing this catalog; all contracts and scenarios are task-authored adaptations. The SHA-256 binds the page text as fetched on the listed date, not a claim that the page is unchanged since.

## PM-S1: Wikipedia: Project management

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_management) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `1ecd28e81786ffc7…`

Surveys project management process groups, including monitoring and controlling, integrated change control, and lessons-learned meetings held to consolidate learnings. Motivates PM01, PM03, PM04, and PM08 as readings of status, change, blocker, and retrospective text. It is motivation for the patterns, not evidence of Jev performance.

## PM-S2: Wikipedia: Risk register

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_register) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `40462237df26bf3e…`

Describes a risk register as a record of identified risks with additional information such as the risk owner and mitigation measures, and defines a trigger as the event that results in the risk occurring. Motivates PM02's owner, trigger, and mitigation check and PM04's reading of what a blocker waits on. Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [PM02]({{ '/project-management/patterns/pm02/' | relative_url }}), [PM04]({{ '/project-management/patterns/pm04/' | relative_url }})

## PM-S3: Wikipedia: Change control

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Change_control) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `257b81fc50733ae2…`

Describes change control as a process of plan and scope, assess, review and approval, build, implement, and close steps so that changes are introduced in a controlled way. Motivates PM03's separation of scope changes from clarifications and PM07's reading of approval replies. Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [PM03]({{ '/project-management/patterns/pm03/' | relative_url }}), [PM07]({{ '/project-management/patterns/pm07/' | relative_url }})

## PM-S4: Wikipedia: Minutes

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minutes) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `5a395aef1948a167…`

States that the primary function of minutes is to record the decisions made, and that a summary of the discussions is distinct from the decisions themselves. Motivates PM05's decision-versus-discussion reading and PM06 and PM07 as readings of what recorded items and replies state. Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [PM05]({{ '/project-management/patterns/pm05/' | relative_url }}), [PM06]({{ '/project-management/patterns/pm06/' | relative_url }}), [PM07]({{ '/project-management/patterns/pm07/' | relative_url }})

## PM-S5: Wikipedia: Action item

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_item) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `0de3debcec51e986…`

Describes an action item as a documented task that can be handled by a single person, typically assigned to someone who is then obligated to perform it and report back, and usually recorded in meeting minutes. Motivates PM06's owner and due expression check. Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.
