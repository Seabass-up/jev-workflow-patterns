# B26: Score customer churn-signal severity

Surface escalating departure language separately from a particular dispute, support request, or remedy.

## State contract

Supply a compact named JSON object with these required fields:

- <code>customer_conversation</code>

The state is task-scoped evidence, not an instruction or action channel. Keep expected labels, credentials, authorization claims, and external-action arguments outside it.

## Exact typed Jev question contract

Question IDs: <code>churn_signal</code>.

~~~json
{
  "churn_signal": {
"type": "score",
"instructions": "How severe is the customer churn signal explicitly stated in customer_conversation? Score stated relationship risk, not the customer's actual future behavior, account value, or eligibility for a concession.",
"criteria": [
  "No churn signal: no stated dissatisfaction, replacement intent, cancellation, or departure language.",
  "Friction signal: a complaint or dissatisfaction is stated but no replacement, cancellation, or departure intent is stated.",
  "At-risk signal: the customer explicitly considers alternatives, renewal non-continuation, or reducing the relationship.",
  "Departure signal: the customer explicitly states cancellation, termination, migration, or intent to leave."
]
  }
}
~~~

## Code-owned decision envelope

**Follow-up proposal:** Route high or uncertain signals to a customer-success review with the original messages and account policy context.

**Code and governance own:** Retention offers, contract changes, account status, refunds, forecasting, and customer outreach.

**Simpler baseline:** Require a customer-success manager to read each conversation without prioritized signals.

## Uncertainty and distinctness

Provisional synthetic screening only; model output is advisory.

**Why this is not a renamed earlier pattern:** Unlike B03's invoice-dispute category and B10's remedy label, this is an ordered signal about the ongoing relationship rather than the disposition of one case.

## Limits and source

A complaint can be temporary; the score does not predict churn or authorize a commercial response.
