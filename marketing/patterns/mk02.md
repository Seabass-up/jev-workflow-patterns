# MK02 — Promotional or transactional content under the team's definition

[Marketing catalog]({{ '/marketing/' | relative_url }}) · [Evaluation and limitations]({{ '/marketing/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) · [Kernel reference module](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Seabass-up/jev-workflow-patterns/main/skills/jev-question-kernel-catalog/references/marketing.md)

Classify whether a message contains promotional content, transactional content, or both, using the organization's own written definition.

This is a proposed, bounded text judgment. Consult the [evaluation record]({{ '/marketing/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) for observed results; the fixture labels below are intended outputs.

## Proposed benefit

Sorts outbound messages by content type so code can apply the organization's sending, consent, and unsubscribe rules; the primary-purpose determination and its legal consequences stay with people.

This is a proposed benefit, not an observed outcome. All fixtures on this page are synthetic.

## Required named state

Supply the following named fields as the evidence packet:

- `classification_rule`
- `message_text`

Code deterministically returns `unknown` without invoking Jev when any required named field is absent or empty; the model is not the required-field gate. The live missing-input case below probes the contract wording, not that gate.

```json
      "transactional_only": "Every part of the message that the rule addresses is transactional content as the rule defines it, and nothing in it is promotional content as the rule defines it.",
      "both_present": "The message contains at least one part that is promotional under the rule and at least one part that is transactional under the rule.",

    }
  }

```

## Policy recorded with its option count

Review when confidence is below 0.8. With 4 options, that floor is equivalent to a top probability of 0.85; a threshold is not portable to a contract with a different option count. These values are provisional workflow policy, not calibration.

## Complete synthetic example

This is fixture `MK02-4`, from the **challenge** split. Its author received the question contract without the design fixtures or model results; the expected label is an authored interpretation of the contract, not a Jev prediction.

```json
{
  "classification_rule": "Promotional content is any text that offers the recipient a discount code, a sale price, a coupon, or a limited-time price reduction. Transactional content is any text that confirms, updates, or completes an order, delivery, or account action the recipient already initiated. Text that does neither is not covered by this rule.",
  "message_text": "Good news: your Cobblewick order has shipped and should arrive on Thursday. While you wait, have a look at our new autumn arrivals at cobblewick.example/autumn; the wool throws are our favourites this year. If you need to change your delivery address, reply to this message before Wednesday noon."
}
```

Expected label: `transactional_only`.

The shipping confirmation and the address-change instruction are transactional under the rule, and the new-arrivals plug offers no discount code, sale price, coupon, or price reduction, so under the supplied definition it is not promotional even though general marketing sense would call it one. The contract requires applying only the supplied rule, and every part the rule addresses is transactional.

## Code-owned responsibilities

Code supplies the current revision of the organization's definition, applies the sending, consent, and unsubscribe requirements for each label, and sends both_present messages to a person who decides the primary purpose. The label never decides what any law requires.

The judgment is advisory. Code and the responsible person retain control of exact identifiers, data access, state changes, and consequential verification.

## Bounded follow-up

None; both_present and unknown messages go to a person.

## Verification recipe

Compare labels with the compliance team's classification of a month of outbound templates; review every message the label called transactional_only that the team classified otherwise.

This describes required verification work, not checks already performed.

## Source inspiration

- [FTC: CAN-SPAM Act: A Compliance Guide for Business](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) (MK-S2). Distinguishes commercial messages, whose primary purpose is advertising or promotion, from transactional or relationship messages, and notes that messages mixing both kinds of content are judged by primary purpose. Motivates MK02 as a reading of which kinds of content appear under the team's own written definition; the primary-purpose determination and the law's requirements stay with people. Motivation for the design, not evidence of Jev performance.

These sources motivate the design problem and its boundaries. They do not demonstrate Jev performance on this question.
