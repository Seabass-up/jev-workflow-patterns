# B27: Classify advertising-claim substantiation posture

Make a campaign claim's supplied support posture reviewable without treating a model result as legal clearance.

## State contract

Supply a compact named JSON object with these required fields:

- <code>campaign_claim</code>
- <code>evidence_inventory</code>

The state is task-scoped evidence, not an instruction or action channel. Keep expected labels, credentials, authorization claims, and external-action arguments outside it.

## Exact typed Jev question contract

Question IDs: <code>substantiation</code>.

~~~json
{
  "substantiation": {
"type": "choice",
"instructions": "How is campaign_claim substantiated by the supplied evidence_inventory? Assess only the named support posture. Do not determine legal compliance, verify study quality, approve publication, or infer evidence not supplied.",
"criteria": {
  "documented_support": "The inventory identifies a directly relevant named record that supports the stated measurable claim.",
  "qualified_but_missing_proof": "The claim is explicitly limited or qualified, but the supplied inventory lacks the named support needed to substantiate it.",
  "unqualified_claim": "The claim makes a measurable performance or comparative assertion without an explicit qualifying limitation or identified support.",
  "not_measurable_claim": "The text is opinion, branding, or otherwise does not state a measurable claim covered by these categories."
}
  }
}
~~~

## Code-owned decision envelope

**Follow-up proposal:** Send all non-documented or low-confidence classifications to marketing/compliance review with source IDs.

**Code and governance own:** Legal analysis, factual verification, evidence retention, approval, publication, and advertising delivery.

**Simpler baseline:** Require manual evidence-packet reading for every campaign assertion.

## Uncertainty and distinctness

Provisional synthetic screening only; model output is advisory.

**Why this is not a renamed earlier pattern:** Unlike B04's proposed-work scope comparison and B12's approval-evidence label, this evaluates a campaign assertion against a supplied evidence inventory without approving publication.

## Limits and source

It cannot establish truth, regulatory compliance, claim scope outside the supplied copy, or publication authority.
