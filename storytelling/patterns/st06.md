# ST06 — Later passage pays off the setup

[Storytelling catalog]({{ '/storytelling/' | relative_url }}) · [Evaluation and limitations]({{ '/storytelling/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) · [Kernel reference module](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Seabass-up/jev-workflow-patterns/main/skills/jev-question-kernel-catalog/references/storytelling.md)

Check whether a later passage resolves a specific setup planted earlier.

This is a proposed, bounded text judgment. Consult the [evaluation record]({{ '/storytelling/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) for observed results; the fixture labels below are intended outputs.

## Proposed benefit

Helps track planted details so they are paid off or removed before publication.

This is a proposed benefit, not an observed outcome. All fixtures on this page are synthetic.

## Required named state

Supply the following named fields as the evidence packet:

- `setup_text`
- `later_passage`

Code deterministically returns `unknown` without invoking Jev when any required named field is absent or empty; the model is not the required-field gate. The live missing-input case below probes the contract wording, not that gate.

## Policy recorded with its option count

Review when confidence is below 0.8. With 3 options, that floor is equivalent to a top probability of 0.8667; a threshold is not portable to a contract with a different option count. These values are provisional workflow policy, not calibration.

## Complete synthetic example

This is fixture `ST06-4`, from the **challenge** split. Its author received the question contract without the design fixtures or model results; the expected label is an authored interpretation of the contract, not a Jev prediction.

```json
{
  "setup_text": "Before he went down to the boats, Jory took the tin lamp off its hook and put it in Elke's hands. 'Keep this in the window,' he said. 'I'll be back before the first snow, and I'll want to see it from the water.'",
  "later_passage": "The snow came on a Tuesday, early and light, and by noon it had stopped being light. Elke watched it fill the ruts in the lane and cover the woodpile and then the top rail of the gate. At dusk she lit the tin lamp as she had every night since the boats went out and set it in the window, and she stood there until the road was gone under white. Then she took the lamp down, blew it out, and put it back on its hook, and she did not light it the next evening, or the one after that."
}
```

Expected label: `paid_off`.

The setup is a promise to return before the first snow, and the later passage breaks that promise on the page: the first snow arrives, Jory does not, and Elke retires the lamp he asked her to keep lit; the contract counts a promise 'kept or broken on the page' as a payoff, so this is not a mere mention even though the promise is not fulfilled.

## Code-owned responsibilities

Code keeps the ledger of setups with their locations, pairs each with candidate payoff passages, and records the author's decision to pay off or remove an unpaid setup.

The judgment is advisory. Code and the responsible person retain control of exact identifiers, data access, state changes, and consequential verification.

## Bounded follow-up

One further candidate passage may be checked when the first is not_paid_off.

## Verification recipe

Have the author list intended payoffs for a draft; compare which candidate passages the label accepts with the author's list.

This describes required verification work, not checks already performed.

## Source inspiration

- [Wikipedia: Chekhov's gun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chekhov%27s_gun) (SS-5). Describes the principle that a detail introduced in a story should be used later. Motivates SL06 and SL08.
- [Aristotle, Poetics (Project Gutenberg)](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/1974/pg1974.txt) (SS-1). Plot as the arrangement of incidents in which each event follows from what precedes it, with reversal and recognition as changes of situation. Motivates SL01 and SL06 as tests of whether a scene changes anything and whether a planted element is used.

These sources motivate the design problem and its boundaries. They do not demonstrate Jev performance on this question.
