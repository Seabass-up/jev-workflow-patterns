# Inspiration, not a performance claim

The design pass read these public pages. Each summary describes the design need, not Jev's effectiveness. No source is represented as endorsing this catalog; all contracts and scenarios are task-authored adaptations. The SHA-256 binds the page text as fetched on the listed date, not a claim that the page is unchanged since.

## HR-S1: Wikipedia: Job description

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Job_description) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `8bd21984cd919794…`

Describes a job description as a written narrative of a position's duties that may specify the qualifications or skills needed, the reporting line, working conditions, and a salary range, and notes its use in recruitment and selection. Motivates HR01 as a reading of how a requirement line is marked and HR06 as a completeness check of the elements a hiring document states. Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.

## HR-S2: U.S. Department of Labor: Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA)

[Read the source](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fmla) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `b80a23375ab04669…`

The Department of Labor's overview of the FMLA: eligible employees may take unpaid, job-protected leave for listed qualifying family and medical reasons, group health benefits continue during leave, and employees request leave by communicating their leave needs to the employer, who responds with specific notices. Motivates HR02 as routing of leave, benefits, and payroll questions to the team that owns the answer and HR08 as classification of a leave request among other requests; eligibility and entitlement stay with people and code. Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [HR02]({{ '/human-resources/patterns/hr02/' | relative_url }}), [HR08]({{ '/human-resources/patterns/hr08/' | relative_url }})

## HR-S3: U.S. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission: Prohibited Employment Policies/Practices

[Read the source](https://www.eeoc.gov/prohibited-employment-policiespractices) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `96fa73f504d00ad0…`

Lists employment practices in which discrimination on protected bases is unlawful, from job advertisements and hiring through pay, benefits, discipline, harassment, and retaliation, and notes that neutral policies with disparate effect must be job-related and necessary to the business. Motivates the collection's boundary that no label classifies a person's protected characteristics, HR01 as separating essential from preferred wording so a person can review essential requirements, HR02 as routing of pay, benefits, and conduct questions, and HR04 as intake routing of treatment, harassment, and retaliation concerns to the organization's own process. Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [HR01]({{ '/human-resources/patterns/hr01/' | relative_url }}), [HR02]({{ '/human-resources/patterns/hr02/' | relative_url }}), [HR04]({{ '/human-resources/patterns/hr04/' | relative_url }})

## HR-S4: Wikipedia: Performance appraisal

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Performance_appraisal) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `75e551e8e8ffad47…`

Describes performance appraisal as periodic documentation and evaluation of an employee's job performance, contrasts the critical-incident technique of recording specific behaviors with rating errors such as the halo effect in which a rater's overall impression colors judgments, and describes behaviorally anchored rating scales. Motivates HR03 and HR07 as readings of whether a note or comment describes a specific instance or states only an impression. Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [HR03]({{ '/human-resources/patterns/hr03/' | relative_url }}), [HR07]({{ '/human-resources/patterns/hr07/' | relative_url }})

## HR-S5: Wikipedia: Exit interview

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exit_interview) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `a4a7e9673133c4b4…`

Describes an exit interview as a survey at separation whose common questions cover reasons for leaving, job satisfaction, and feedback, with questions relating to the work environment, supervisors, compensation, the work itself, and company culture, used in aggregate to reduce turnover. Motivates HR05 as classification of the theme an exit comment gives. Motivation only, not evidence of Jev performance.
