# B24: Classify competitive-displacement evidence

Separate named competitive context from vague comparison language before a sales workflow prepares research.

## State contract

Supply a compact named JSON object with these required fields:

- <code>sales_note</code>

The state is task-scoped evidence, not an instruction or action channel. Keep expected labels, credentials, authorization claims, and external-action arguments outside it.

## Exact typed Jev question contract

Question IDs: <code>competitive_signal</code>.

~~~json
{
  "competitive_signal": {
"type": "choice",
"instructions": "What competitive-displacement evidence is explicitly stated in sales_note? Do not infer a competitor from market knowledge, create a competitive record, or decide how to respond.",
"criteria": {
  "named_current_competitor": "The note explicitly identifies another provider currently used or evaluated by the account.",
  "generic_comparison": "The note only asks for a comparison or mentions alternatives without identifying a current competing provider.",
  "replacement_or_loss_event": "The note explicitly says the account is replacing a provider or that business was lost to a named or described alternative.",
  "none_or_unclear": "No supplied language establishes one of the other competitive evidence states."
}
  }
}
~~~

## Code-owned decision envelope

**Follow-up proposal:** Preserve the exact note and ask the account owner for confirmed competitor context when the signal is generic or unclear.

**Code and governance own:** Competitor identity resolution, market research, pricing, messaging, CRM writes, and outreach.

**Simpler baseline:** Ask an account owner to categorize every mention of alternatives manually.

## Uncertainty and distinctness

Provisional synthetic screening only; model output is advisory.

**Why this is not a renamed earlier pattern:** Unlike B16's price-formation label, this identifies the commercial relationship to alternatives and does not classify a quoted price.

## Limits and source

A sales note may be speculative and does not prove market position, contractual replacement, or a competing vendor's capabilities.
