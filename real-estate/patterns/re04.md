# RE04 — Topic a lease clause governs

[Real estate catalog]({{ '/real-estate/' | relative_url }}) · [Evaluation and limitations]({{ '/real-estate/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) · [Kernel reference module](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Seabass-up/jev-workflow-patterns/main/skills/jev-question-kernel-catalog/references/real-estate.md)

Classify which of four topics one lease clause primarily governs so a lease summary or review queue can be assembled.

This is a proposed, bounded text judgment. Consult the [evaluation record]({{ '/real-estate/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) for observed results; the fixture labels below are intended outputs.

<div class="callout warning"><p><strong>Provisional.</strong> At least one screening case disagreed with its authored label or did not complete. The disagreement is preserved on the evaluation page; neither the contract nor the label was changed after inference.</p></div>

## Proposed benefit

Lets the leasing office assemble a clause-by-clause summary from any lease format without deciding what a clause means legally.

This is a proposed benefit, not an observed outcome. All fixtures on this page are synthetic.

- `lease_clause`

Review when confidence is below 0.8. With 5 options, that floor is equivalent to a top probability of 0.84; a threshold is not portable to a contract with a different option count. These values are provisional workflow policy, not calibration.

## Complete synthetic example

This is fixture `RE04-4`, from the **challenge** split. Its author received the question contract without the design fixtures or model results; the expected label is an authored interpretation of the contract, not a Jev prediction.

```json
{
  "lease_clause": "Tenant shall not make alterations, install fixtures, or change or add locks without Landlord's prior written consent. Any alteration Landlord approves shall be maintained by Tenant in good repair for the term of the lease, and Tenant shall repair at Tenant's expense any damage to the premises the alteration causes."
}
```

Expected label: `maintenance`.

The clause covers use (restricting alterations and lock changes) and maintenance (assigning responsibility to maintain and repair). Both precedence orders the contract states list maintenance before use, so maintenance applies even though the clause opens as an alterations restriction.

## Code-owned responsibilities

Code splits the lease into clauses, records the lease identifier and revision, and maps the label to a summary section or review queue; whether a clause is enforceable or compliant with local law stays with counsel.

The judgment is advisory. Code and the responsible person retain control of exact identifiers, data access, state changes, and consequential verification.

## Bounded follow-up

None; unknown clauses go to the leasing manager.

## Verification recipe

Compare labels with the topic headings in leases that already carry them; review clauses where the label and the heading disagree.

This describes required verification work, not checks already performed.
