# Inspiration, not a performance claim

The design pass read these public pages. Each summary describes the design need, not Jev's effectiveness. No source is represented as endorsing this catalog; all contracts and scenarios are task-authored adaptations. The SHA-256 binds the page text as fetched on the listed date, not a claim that the page is unchanged since.

## CE-S1: Wikipedia: Memorandum

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memorandum) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `63b616285ba52e43…`

Describes memos and briefing notes as documents that bring a problem to notice, are marked either for information or for decision, and typically give background, identify options, and advocate a recommended one. Motivates CE01 and CE05 as readings of whether a memo states its decision and what kind of response it asks for. This is motivation for the questions, not evidence of Jev performance.

## CE-S2: Wikipedia: Business case

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_case) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `4c4c71bd2cc5d5ef…`

Describes a formal business case as including background, expected benefits, the options considered with reasons for rejecting or carrying forward each, expected costs and risks, and consideration of doing nothing, and lists measurable key aspects among the marks of a good case. Motivates CE04, CE06, and CE07 as completeness readings of proposal text. This is motivation for the questions, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [CE02]({{ '/executive/patterns/ce02/' | relative_url }}), [CE03]({{ '/executive/patterns/ce03/' | relative_url }}), [CE04]({{ '/executive/patterns/ce04/' | relative_url }}), [CE06]({{ '/executive/patterns/ce06/' | relative_url }}), [CE07]({{ '/executive/patterns/ce07/' | relative_url }})

## CE-S3: Wikipedia: Anecdotal evidence

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anecdotal_evidence) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `b0c65bae587b3b00…`

Describes anecdotal evidence as personal experiences, self-reported claims, and eyewitness accounts collected non-systematically, and notes that one or a few anecdotes risk being unrepresentative. Motivates CE03 as a classification of the kind of evidence a proposal offers, distinct from whether the evidence is correct. This is motivation for the question, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [CE03]({{ '/executive/patterns/ce03/' | relative_url }})

## CE-S4: Wikipedia: Real options valuation

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_options_valuation) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `7683b3d2def80d69…`

Describes management flexibility to defer, abandon, expand, stage, or contract a project as valuable under uncertainty, in contrast with irreversible investment. Motivates CE02 as a reading of whether a proposal describes its own commitment as reversible or one-way; the model performs no valuation. This is motivation for the question, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [CE02]({{ '/executive/patterns/ce02/' | relative_url }})

## CE-S5: Wikipedia: Management by exception

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management_by_exception) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `538043c028a224f6…`

Describes a management style in which routine matters are handled at lower levels and only cases that deviate from the norm or exceed set limits are brought to management's attention. Motivates CE08 as a check of an escalation request against the organization's stated escalation conditions, with any numeric limit compared in code. This is motivation for the question, not evidence of Jev performance.
