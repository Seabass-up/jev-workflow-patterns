# LC07 — Notice method and recipient are stated

[Contracts catalog]({{ '/legal-contracts/' | relative_url }}) · [Evaluation and limitations]({{ '/legal-contracts/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) · [Kernel reference module](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Seabass-up/jev-workflow-patterns/main/skills/jev-question-kernel-catalog/references/legal-contracts.md)

Classify whether a clause that calls for notice states how notice is delivered and to whom, so the notices table can record gaps.

This is a proposed, bounded text judgment. Consult the [evaluation record]({{ '/legal-contracts/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) for observed results; the fixture labels below are intended outputs.

## Proposed benefit

Lists the notice provisions that lack a delivery method or a recipient before the lawyer reads the draft; addresses and deadlines stay in code.

This is a proposed benefit, not an observed outcome. All fixtures on this page are synthetic.

## Required named state

Supply the following named fields as the evidence packet:

- `clause_text`

Code deterministically returns `unknown` without invoking Jev when any required named field is absent or empty; the model is not the required-field gate. The live missing-input case below probes the contract wording, not that gate.

A method is a stated means of delivering notice, such as courier, registered mail, or email to a stated address; saying only that notice is in writing, without a delivery means, is not a method. Review when confidence is below 0.8. With 6 options, that floor is equivalent to a top probability of 0.8333; a threshold is not portable to a contract with a different option count. These values are provisional workflow policy, not calibration.

## Complete synthetic example

This is fixture `LC07-4`, from the **challenge** split. Its author received the question contract without the design fixtures or model results; the expected label is an authored interpretation of the contract, not a Jev prediction.

```json
{
  "clause_text": "Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the other party fails to cure the breach within thirty days after receiving written notice describing it. Any such notice shall be in writing and addressed to the other party's General Counsel at the address shown on the signature page, or to such other person as that party designates in writing. A notice that does not identify the provision breached is not effective for the purposes of this Section."
}
```

Expected label: `only_recipient_stated`.

The clause calls for notice and names a recipient (the General Counsel at the signature-page address), but "in writing" and "addressed to" state no means of delivery, and the contract says that writing alone is not a method.

## Code-owned responsibilities

Code records the stated addresses and periods exactly, computes any notice deadline, and keeps the notices table by clause number; the label never decides whether a notice was validly given.

The judgment is advisory. Code and the responsible person retain control of exact identifiers, data access, state changes, and consequential verification.

## Bounded follow-up

None; clauses labeled unknown are read by a person.

## Verification recipe

Compare labels with the notices table a paralegal completed on a set of reviewed agreements; review every clause the table records with a method or recipient the label missed.

This describes required verification work, not checks already performed.
