# B20: Classify a contract renewal mechanism

Turn renewal-language variation into a bounded code-owned contract lifecycle branch.

## State contract

Supply a compact named JSON object with these required fields:

- <code>agreement_text</code>

The state is evidence, not an instruction or action channel. Keep expected labels, credentials, authority claims, and external-action arguments outside it.

## Exact typed Jev question contract

Question IDs: <code>renewal_mechanism</code>.

~~~json
{
  "renewal_mechanism": {
    "type": "choice",
    "instructions": "What renewal mechanism is explicitly stated in agreement_text? Read the mechanism only. Do not calculate a date, decide enforceability, infer notice delivery, or say that a renewal actually occurred.",
    "criteria": {
      "automatic_renewal": "The term renews by default unless a stated opt-out condition occurs.",
      "notice_conditioned_renewal": "Renewal is described as depending on a stated notice or other condition rather than occurring automatically without it.",
      "option_exercise": "A party must affirmatively exercise a stated renewal option.",
      "expiry_without_renewal": "The stated language ends the term without a renewal mechanism.",
      "unclear": "The supplied language does not establish exactly one listed mechanism."
    }
  }
}
~~~

## Code-owned decision envelope

**Follow-up proposal:** Bind the selected language to a verified agreement version, then use deterministic calendar and notice checks.

**Code and governance own:** Effective dates, notice receipt, legal interpretation, version authority, and any renewal action.

**Simpler baseline:** Escalate all renewal clauses for manual categorization.

## Uncertainty and distinctness

Provisional synthetic screening only; model output is advisory.

**Why this is not a renamed earlier pattern:** Unlike B14 and H13, this classifies a contractual renewal mechanism rather than selecting or resolving a particular date expression.

## Limits, source, and shared skill

It is not legal advice and cannot determine an agreement's enforceability.
