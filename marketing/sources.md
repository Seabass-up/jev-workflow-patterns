# Inspiration, not a performance claim

The design pass read these public pages. Each summary describes the design need, not Jev's effectiveness. No source is represented as endorsing this catalog; all contracts and scenarios are task-authored adaptations. The SHA-256 binds the page text as fetched on the listed date, not a claim that the page is unchanged since.

## MK-S1: FTC: Advertising FAQ's: A Guide for Small Business

[Read the source](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `8c452c12b9c6a2d3…`

Explains that advertisers need a reasonable basis of objective evidence for claims before running an ad, that satisfied-customer letters are not enough for claims requiring objective evidence, and that comparative advertising naming a competitor is permitted when truthful and substantiated. Motivates MK01 as a reading of whether a claim matches its supplied evidence and MK06 as a reading of whether a competitor mention is comparative; this page is motivation for the question design, not evidence of Jev performance, and the reasonable-basis and legality determinations stay with people.

## MK-S2: FTC: CAN-SPAM Act: A Compliance Guide for Business

[Read the source](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `8121f4f0414abd24…`

Distinguishes commercial messages, whose primary purpose is advertising or promotion, from transactional or relationship messages, and notes that messages mixing both kinds of content are judged by primary purpose. Motivates MK02 as a reading of which kinds of content appear under the team's own written definition; the primary-purpose determination and the law's requirements stay with people. Motivation for the design, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [MK02]({{ '/marketing/patterns/mk02/' | relative_url }})

## MK-S3: Wikipedia: Call to action (marketing)

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_to_action_(marketing)) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `e69927a3f41e96f6…`

Describes a call to action as the part of a marketing message that asks the audience to take a specific action, such as buying, subscribing, or visiting. Motivates MK03 as a count of distinct asks in copy; encyclopedic overview offered as motivation, not evidence of Jev performance.

## MK-S4: Wikipedia: Creative brief

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_brief) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `460d95549c3477f5…`

Describes the creative brief as the document that sets out a campaign's objective, target audience, key message, and how results will be judged before creative work begins, and as the reference point reviewers use when evaluating the resulting work. Motivates MK07 as a presence check for goal, audience, and metric, MK04 as a reading of copy against the brief's stated audience, and MK08 as a reading of whether a review comment challenges the brief's intent or the piece's execution. Motivation for the design, not evidence of Jev performance.

Used by: [MK04]({{ '/marketing/patterns/mk04/' | relative_url }}), [MK07]({{ '/marketing/patterns/mk07/' | relative_url }}), [MK08]({{ '/marketing/patterns/mk08/' | relative_url }})
