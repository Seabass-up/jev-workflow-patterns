# Inspiration, not a performance claim

The design pass read these public pages. Each summary describes the design need, not Jev's effectiveness. No source is represented as endorsing this catalog; all contracts and scenarios are task-authored adaptations. The SHA-256 binds the page text as fetched on the listed date, not a claim that the page is unchanged since.

## FN-S1: Wikipedia: Accounts payable

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accounts_payable) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `f801ccc92384f570…`

Describes the accounts payable function: invoices received from vendors, matched to purchase orders and receiving records, approved, and paid, with supplier statements and approved expense reports as supporting documentation. Motivates FN01, FN02, FN05, FN06, FN07, and FN08 as readings of the documents and messages that function handles; encyclopedic motivation, not evidence of Jev performance.

## FN-S2: Wikipedia: Invoice

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invoice) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `5c8539a674bc2862…`

## FN-S4: Wikipedia: Remittance advice

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remittance_advice) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `7f646cc37b1a6ede…`

Describes remittance advice as a payer's notice of a payment and the invoices it covers, used to match payments to invoices. Motivates FN05 as a message-intent reading and FN08 as a memo-consistency reading; encyclopedic motivation, not evidence of Jev performance.

## FN-S5: Wikipedia: Variance (accounting)

[Read the source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variance_(accounting)) · checked 2026-09-23 · SHA-256 `8575d3fa21e7d46e…`

Describes budget-versus-actual and standard-cost variances, favorable and unfavorable directions, and variance analysis as the practice of explaining them. Motivates FN04 as a check on whether commentary explains a variance and FN06 as a reading of budget requests; the variance itself is computed in code. Encyclopedic motivation, not evidence of Jev performance.
