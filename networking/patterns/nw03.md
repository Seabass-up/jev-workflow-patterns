# NW03 — Maintenance notice states who will be affected

[Networking catalog]({{ '/networking/' | relative_url }}) · [Evaluation and limitations]({{ '/networking/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) · [Kernel reference module](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Seabass-up/jev-workflow-patterns/main/skills/jev-question-kernel-catalog/references/networking.md)

Check whether a maintenance notice tells its readers which sites, circuits, services, or users the work will affect and what they will experience.

This is a proposed, bounded text judgment. Consult the [evaluation record]({{ '/networking/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) for observed results; the fixture labels below are intended outputs.

## Proposed benefit

Returns notices that only name the equipment being worked on to their author before recipients have to ask whether they are affected.

This is a proposed benefit, not an observed outcome. All fixtures on this page are synthetic.

## Required named state

Supply the following named fields as the evidence packet:

- `maintenance_notice`

Code deterministically returns `unknown` without invoking Jev when any required named field is absent or empty; the model is not the required-field gate. The live missing-input case below probes the contract wording, not that gate.

## Policy recorded with its option count

Review when confidence is below 0.8. With 4 options, that floor is equivalent to a top probability of 0.85; a threshold is not portable to a contract with a different option count. These values are provisional workflow policy, not calibration.

## Complete synthetic example

This is fixture `NW03-4`, from the **challenge** split. Its author received the question contract without the design fixtures or model results; the expected label is an authored interpretation of the contract, not a Jev prediction.

```json
{
  "maintenance_notice": "Firmware upgrade on the Riverside edge router pair, Saturday 01:00 to 03:00 local time. The routers will be upgraded one at a time and the procedure is expected to be hitless; no user-visible interruption is expected for Riverside staff or for internet access. Note that the site-to-site tunnel to Harbor Point will drop for roughly two minutes during each failover, so Harbor Point users will briefly lose access to applications hosted at Riverside."
}
```

Expected label: `affected_scope_stated`.

The notice states no impact for Riverside users but an expected two-minute interruption for Harbor Point users and says what they will experience. The contract says a notice that states an interruption for some scope and no impact for other scopes is affected_scope_stated, so the prominent no-impact language does not yield no_impact_expected.

## Code-owned responsibilities

Code owns window times, the mapping from named circuits and sites to customers, and the notification list; a notice labeled scope_not_stated goes back to its author for the missing statement.

The judgment is advisory. Code and the responsible person retain control of exact identifiers, data access, state changes, and consequential verification.

## Bounded follow-up

None; a notice without a stated scope is returned to its author rather than re-asked.

## Verification recipe

Compare labels with recipient questions after past notices; count notices labeled affected_scope_stated that still drew replies asking whether the reader was affected.

This describes required verification work, not checks already performed.

## Source inspiration

- [Wikipedia: Maintenance window](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maintenance_window) (NW-S3). Describes a maintenance window as a period announced in advance during which preventive maintenance that could disrupt service may be performed, so that clients can prepare for possible disruption. Motivates NW03 as a check that a notice states who or what will be affected; window times and customer mappings stay in code. This is motivation for the pattern, not evidence of Jev performance.

These sources motivate the design problem and its boundaries. They do not demonstrate Jev performance on this question.
