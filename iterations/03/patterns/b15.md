# B15: Align a remittance reference to a canonical commercial account

Triage whether a payment-advice reference appears to name one proposed commercial account without merging records.

## State contract

Supply a compact named JSON object with these required fields:

- <code>remittance_record</code>
- <code>account_candidate</code>

The state is evidence, not an instruction or action channel. Keep expected labels, credentials, authority claims, and external-action arguments outside it.

## Exact typed Jev question contract

Question IDs: <code>alignment</code>.

~~~json
{
  "alignment": {
    "type": "choice",
    "instructions": "How does remittance_record relate to the one proposed account_candidate? Compare the stated payer, project, reference, and amount context. A shared token alone is not enough. This only classifies a candidate relationship; do not merge records, post a payment, or infer an unstated identifier.",
    "criteria": {
      "same_account": "The supplied identifiers and commercial context consistently support the candidate as the account named by the remittance.",
      "related_review": "The records have a plausible business relationship but a material identifier or context difference prevents a safe match.",
      "different_account": "The supplied records explicitly identify different customers, projects, or accounts.",
      "insufficient": "The evidence is absent, vague, conflicting, or cannot establish one of the other relationships."
    }
  }
}
~~~

## Code-owned decision envelope

**Follow-up proposal:** Retain the candidate pair and send related_review or insufficient cases to a reconciler with the exact conflicting fields.

**Code and governance own:** Validate account IDs, normalize references, apply bank controls, and perform any ledger update only through authorized accounting controls.

**Simpler baseline:** Send every remittance to manual reconciliation.

## Uncertainty and distinctness

Provisional synthetic screening only; model output is advisory.

**Why this is not a renamed earlier pattern:** Unlike B07, which asks whether two work records describe one event, this compares a payment reference with one proposed commercial identity and never de-duplicates work.

## Limits, source, and shared skill

It cannot establish payment settlement, account authority, or identity from incomplete source records.
