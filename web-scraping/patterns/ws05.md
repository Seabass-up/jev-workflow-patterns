# WS05 — Record contains personal data the supplied policy excludes

[Web data catalog]({{ '/web-scraping/' | relative_url }}) · [Evaluation and limitations]({{ '/web-scraping/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) · [Kernel reference module](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Seabass-up/jev-workflow-patterns/main/skills/jev-question-kernel-catalog/references/web-scraping.md)

Classify whether an extracted record contains personal data that the team's supplied policy says to exclude from the dataset.

This is a proposed, bounded text judgment. Consult the [evaluation record]({{ '/web-scraping/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) for observed results; the fixture labels below are intended outputs.

## Proposed benefit

Applies the team's own written exclusion rules to each record consistently before storage, without the model deciding what any law requires.

This is a proposed benefit, not an observed outcome. All fixtures on this page are synthetic.

## Required named state

Supply the following named fields as the evidence packet:

- `exclusion_policy`
- `record`

Code deterministically returns `unknown` without invoking Jev when any required named field is absent or empty; the model is not the required-field gate. The live missing-input case below probes the contract wording, not that gate.

```json
{
  "decision": {
    "type": "choice",
    "instructions": "First, if any required field (`exclusion_policy`, `record`) is absent, empty, or lacks the information needed for this judgment, select unknown before considering other labels. `exclusion_policy` is the team's written rule about which kinds of personal data must be excluded from collected records and which kinds may be kept. `record` is one extracted record. Classify the record against that policy. A record holding any information about an identifiable individual of a kind the policy excludes contains excluded data. A record whose only personal data is of a kind the policy allows to be kept holds allowed personal data. A record about an organization, a product, a place, or an event with no information about an identifiable individual holds no personal data.     "criteria": {

      "no_personal_data": "The record concerns an organization, product, place, or event and holds no information about an identifiable individual.",

    }
```

## Policy recorded with its option count

Review when confidence is below 0.8. With 4 options, that floor is equivalent to a top probability of 0.85; a threshold is not portable to a contract with a different option count. These values are provisional workflow policy, not calibration.

## Complete synthetic example

This is fixture `WS05-4`, from the **challenge** split. Its author received the question contract without the design fixtures or model results; the expected label is an authored interpretation of the contract, not a Jev prediction.

```json
{
  "exclusion_policy": "Keep: the name, job title, and work contact details of a person named in an official capacity, such as an event organizer, press contact, or listed agent, where work contact details means an email address or phone number at the organization's own domain or switchboard. Exclude: home addresses, personal mobile numbers, email addresses at general consumer mail providers or otherwise not at the organization's own domain, dates of birth, photographs of people, and any information about a person under 18. Apply this to every field of the record.",
  "record": "Event: Riverbend Autumn Lantern Walk. Organizer: Riverbend Arts Trust (riverbendarts.example). Contact: Dana Reyes, Programme Lead, dana.reyes.lanterns@freemail.example. Venue: Riverbend Park, north gate. Date: 24 October 2026, 18:30. Free entry; lanterns supplied."
}
```

Expected label: `contains_excluded_data`.

Dana Reyes is named in an official capacity, and the name and title are of an allowed kind, but the contact email is at a consumer mail provider rather than the organization's own domain, which the policy excludes. The contract selects contains_excluded_data when allowed and excluded kinds are both present.

## Code-owned responsibilities

Code drops or redacts the fields the policy names exactly, records the policy revision applied, holds any record labeled contains_excluded_data or unknown from storage, and keeps legal review of the policy itself with people.

The judgment is advisory. Code and the responsible person retain control of exact identifiers, data access, state changes, and consequential verification.

## Bounded follow-up

None; held records go to the privacy reviewer.

## Verification recipe

Have the privacy reviewer mark a sample of records under the same policy and compare; measure excluded data the label missed first.

This describes required verification work, not checks already performed.
