# B23: Score stated purchase-intent strength

Expose the strength of language about buying without inventing deal value or forecasting revenue.

## State contract

Supply a compact named JSON object with these required fields:

- <code>prospect_message</code>

The state is task-scoped evidence, not an instruction or action channel. Keep expected labels, credentials, authorization claims, and external-action arguments outside it.

## Exact typed Jev question contract

Question IDs: <code>intent_strength</code>.

~~~json
{
  "intent_strength": {
"type": "score",
"instructions": "How strong is the purchase intent explicitly stated in prospect_message? Score language, not account value, probability of close, or a promise by another party.",
"criteria": [
  "No commercial intent: support, casual discussion, or no stated interest in acquiring the offering.",
  "Exploration: asks general capability, pricing, or availability questions without a stated evaluation or next buying step.",
  "Active evaluation: compares options, asks implementation/procurement questions, or states a defined evaluation process.",
  "Explicit commitment signal: states an intended order, approval, contract step, or immediate purchase action subject only to named administrative conditions."
]
  }
}
~~~

## Code-owned decision envelope

**Follow-up proposal:** Review borderline scores with the message text and ask one missing commercial question rather than escalating a numeric score into a deal forecast.

**Code and governance own:** Forecasting, lead scoring weights, pricing, CRM writes, opportunity creation, and outreach actions.

**Simpler baseline:** Require a human to read every inbound prospect message before prioritization.

## Uncertainty and distinctness

Provisional synthetic screening only; model output is advisory.

**Why this is not a renamed earlier pattern:** Unlike B01's request routing and B10's requested remedy, this is an ordered measurement of prospect purchase language rather than a handler or remedy label.

## Limits and source

Intent language is not a signed commitment, a credit decision, or a reliable revenue forecast.
