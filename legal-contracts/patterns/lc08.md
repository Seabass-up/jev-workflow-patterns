# LC08 — Clause states an exception to its own rule

[Contracts catalog]({{ '/legal-contracts/' | relative_url }}) · [Evaluation and limitations]({{ '/legal-contracts/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) · [Kernel reference module](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Seabass-up/jev-workflow-patterns/main/skills/jev-question-kernel-catalog/references/legal-contracts.md)

Classify whether a clause states a general rule and carves cases out of it, refers to exceptions stated elsewhere, states a rule with no exception, or states no rule at all.

This is a proposed, bounded text judgment. Consult the [evaluation record]({{ '/legal-contracts/evaluation/' | relative_url }}) for observed results; the fixture labels below are intended outputs.

## Proposed benefit

Lists every carve-out and every cross-referenced exception for the lawyer to check, without a person scanning each clause for exception language.

This is a proposed benefit, not an observed outcome. All fixtures on this page are synthetic.

## Required named state

Supply the following named fields as the evidence packet:

- `clause_text`

Code deterministically returns `unknown` without invoking Jev when any required named field is absent or empty; the model is not the required-field gate. The live missing-input case below probes the contract wording, not that gate.

An exception carves cases, parties, or circumstances out of that rule, often introduced by words such as except, unless, other than, provided that, or does not apply to. ## Complete synthetic example

This is fixture `LC08-4`, from the **challenge** split. Its author received the question contract without the design fixtures or model results; the expected label is an authored interpretation of the contract, not a Jev prediction.

```json
{
  "clause_text": "\"Confidential Information\" means all non-public business, technical, and financial information that a party discloses to the other in connection with this Agreement, whether orally, in writing, or by inspection. Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving party, that the receiving party already possessed without restriction, or that the receiving party independently develops without reference to the disclosing party's information."
}
```

Expected label: `no_rule_stated`.

The clause is a definition with exclusions; it states no requirement, prohibition, limit, or right, so its "does not include" language narrows a defined term rather than carving cases out of a rule, and the contract directs no_rule_stated for a definition.

## Code-owned responsibilities

Code resolves any cross-reference to the referenced clause or document exactly and attaches it for the lawyer; the label never interprets what the exception means for either party.

The judgment is advisory. Code and the responsible person retain control of exact identifiers, data access, state changes, and consequential verification.

## Bounded follow-up

None; clauses labeled carve_out_by_reference_only are queued with the referenced text attached by code.

## Verification recipe

Compare labels with a lawyer's list of carve-outs on a set of reviewed agreements; review every carve-out the lawyer listed that the label missed.

This describes required verification work, not checks already performed.

## Source inspiration

- [Wikipedia: Non-disclosure agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-disclosure_agreement) (LC-S2). Classifies confidentiality agreements as unilateral (one-way), bilateral (mutual), or multilateral, and lists typical contents: the definition of what is confidential, the exclusions from what must be kept confidential, and a duty to give the disclosing party prompt notice of compelled disclosure. Motivates LC01 (confidentiality as a clause type), LC03 (reciprocal versus one-sided), LC06 (a defined term and its uses), LC07 (notice elements), and LC08 (carve-outs from a rule). Motivation, not evidence of Jev performance.
- [Wikipedia: Exclusion clause](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exclusion_clause) (LC-S5). Describes clauses that exclude or limit a party's liability and notes that courts read such clauses strictly and against the party relying on them. Motivates LC01 (limitation of liability as a clause type) and LC08 (whether a clause states exceptions to its own rule). How any such clause is enforced is a legal question that stays with the lawyer. Motivation, not evidence of Jev performance.

These sources motivate the design problem and its boundaries. They do not demonstrate Jev performance on this question.
