# H31: Noul threshold-crossing repeat audit

Keep repeated probability samples visible when a policy threshold could change a disposition.

## State contract

Supply a compact named JSON object with these required fields:

- <code>decision_name</code>
- <code>repeat_observations</code>
- <code>policy_thresholds</code>

The state is task-scoped evidence, not an instruction or action channel. Keep expected labels, credentials, authorization claims, and external-action arguments outside it.

## Exact typed Jev question contract

Question IDs: <code>repeatability_status</code>.

~~~json
{
  "repeatability_status": {
"type": "choice",
"instructions": "Given code-produced repeat_observations for one frozen Noul contract and policy_thresholds, how should the semantic stability be described? Do not calculate variance, average values, change thresholds, or take an action.",
"criteria": {
  "stable_away_from_threshold": "The supplied summary says observations consistently remain on one policy side with a stated margin.",
  "near_threshold_review": "The supplied summary says observations remain near a threshold or the margin is too small for the stated policy.",
  "threshold_crossing": "The supplied summary says repeated observations fall on different policy sides.",
  "insufficient": "The supplied observations/threshold summary cannot establish a listed stability state."
}
  }
}
~~~

## Code-owned decision envelope

**Follow-up proposal:** Code computes statistics and policy sides, preserves every raw repeat, and routes crossing/near-threshold cases to review.

**Code and governance own:** Sampling count, variance calculations, thresholds, human review, action policy, and data retention.

**Simpler baseline:** Use a single Noul probability for every thresholded action.

## Uncertainty and distinctness

Provisional synthetic screening only; model output is advisory.

**Why this is not a renamed earlier pattern:** Unlike H12's generic repeated-decision stability gate, this is a Noul-specific controller that preserves raw probabilities and threshold-crossing evidence rather than collapsing them to a single label.

## Limits and source

Repeated synthetic observations do not establish production calibration, stability under data drift, or a universal threshold.
